## Question

What is every option a Design offers, per Design kind, with its range and default?

Cover Outlines (preset list, custom SVG, or Outline-from-Decoration), size, Switch count and placement for a Cap Clicker (1–3?), and key count and layout for a Block Clicker (row, grid, WASD...). Then Decoration types (text with which fonts, SVG upload, PNG trace, an icon library?) and which surfaces each may go on (Top Cap, Keycaps, top surface, sides, underside). Then the technique per surface (raised, flush or engraved), colors and their limit (4 AMS slots per Plate), and the keyring loop.

Start from the parity checklist, then add the extras this effort wants beyond it.

Also decide whether side, edge and underside Decorations are limited to one colour. Per [Geometry stack gaps](02-geometry-stack-gaps.md), a multi-colour side Decoration costs a filament change on every layer it spans.
