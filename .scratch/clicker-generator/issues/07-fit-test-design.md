## Question

What does the Fit Test contain, and how do its results become the Fit Calibration?

Decide which test pieces to include: Switch Pocket fit, stem socket fit for Top Cap and Keycap, Top Cap–Base gap, minimum text height and stroke width, and emboss and engrave depth. Decide the range and step for each (e.g. 13.9–14.2 mm in 0.05 mm steps), and how many fit on one Plate. Decide how the owner records the winners, and what one Fit Calibration is scoped to (printer, filament brand, color).
