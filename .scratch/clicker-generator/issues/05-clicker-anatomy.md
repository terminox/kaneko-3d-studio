## Question

What exactly are the parts of a Cap Clicker and a Block Clicker, and how do they fit together?

Cover the Base, Top Cap and Keycap, and the Switch Pocket (clip-in plate thickness vs. a full pocket). Then the stem socket in the Top Cap or Keycap: how the Top Cap is retained (snap, stem only, guide walls), the gap between Top Cap and Base, and switch travel. Then floor thickness, the keyring loop or hole, and how a Block Clicker's keys are laid out and spaced. Finally, which way up each part prints and which surfaces face the build plate.

Use the reference teardown's parity checklist as the starting point. Update CONTEXT.md as terms sharpen.
